Welcome to Capital

Your data, your folder

Give your savings
a clear purpose.

Track balances. Connect goals. See what is already covered.

1. Choose a folderCreate a dedicated local folder or reopen existing Capital data.

2. Set your currencyEach bucket and goal can use its own.

3. Add your savingsEnter balances manually or track public wallet addresses.

Wallet providers receive the addresses you track. Capital never needs private keys or seed phrases.
