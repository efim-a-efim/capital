## Design

- `Series(key: String, points: Map<String, String>)` (date `YYYY-MM-DD` → decimal), `Portfolio.history: List<Series>`, unique keys, ≤ 4000 points each (≈ 11 years daily), validated like quotes. Keys `rate:<ASSET>` = USD price per unit at the day's close (`USD` itself is never stored).
- **Providers** (`Providers.kt`, same rules as quotes): Frankfurter `https://api.frankfurter.dev/v1/{from}..{to}?base=USD&symbols=…` (chunks of 1 year; `rates[date][symbol]` is units per USD → price = 1/rate); DefiLlama `https://coins.llama.fi/chart/{coins}?start={unix}&span={days}&period=1d` for `coingecko:<id>` natives and `<platform>:<contract>` tokens (`coins[key].prices[].{timestamp,price}`). `history(assets, since)` fills only missing days; failures keep the old series and report `Provider · entity: message`.
- **Collection**: every refresh appends today's quote of every asset to its series (`Portfolio.recorded(today)`); the history fetch runs when a needed day is missing (first run, gaps).
- **Background job**: `RatesJob : JobService`, scheduled with `JobInfo.Builder(id, ComponentName).setPeriodic(period).setRequiredNetworkType(NETWORK_TYPE_ANY).setPersisted(true)`; rescheduled when the setting changes, cancelled on Off. The foreground save writes the asset list and the provider choices to app-private prefs (`rates-job.json`: currency codes and provider names only, no amounts). The job fetches `crypto()`/`fiat()` quotes for that list and appends `{date, asset, usd}` to `rates-cache.json` in `filesDir`; `CapitalModel` merges the cache on open and on refresh (newest sample → `quotes`, each sample → `history`), then clears it. *Call: no folder writes from the job.*
- **Settings UI**: `Choice` **Auto update rates** with Off / 24 h / 12 h / 6 h / 3 h / 1 h; note on battery and data; the time of the last background sample is shown under Quotes and freshness.
- Privacy Policy: background requests carry the same data as a refresh (asset codes, IP).
