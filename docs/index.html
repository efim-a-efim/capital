See when every savings goal closes.

Capital is a private savings tracker for Android. Group your money into buckets, connect them to goals, add the savings you plan to make, and the app tells you which goal is funded, which one closes in time and which one falls short.

No account. No analytics. No ads. Records live in a folder you choose; sync it with any tool you already use.

What it does

  • Buckets

    Cash, savings accounts, brokerage, crypto. Enter quantities by hand or paste a public wallet address and let the app read the balance.

  • Goals with priorities

    Connect buckets to goals with limits. Earlier dates fund first; drag goals with the same date to set their order. Allocation never moves money.

  • Plans

    Add the savings you intend to make. The projection shows the date each goal closes and marks it Funded in time or Not funded.

  • Portfolio mode

    Target percentages per asset, real share versus target, and a rebalance calculator that lists what to buy.

  • Wallets and prices

    BTC, ETH, TON and TRON addresses plus ERC-20, jetton and TRC-20 tokens. Prices from DefiLlama, CoinGecko or CoinPaprika; fiat rates from Frankfurter or the ECB. Every source is selectable.

  • Encryption and lock

    Optional AES-256-GCM encryption of your files with an Argon2id-derived key, PIN and biometric unlock, screenshots blocked while locked data is open.

  • Your folder, your sync

    Snapshots are plain files with revision ids and checksums. Sync them with Syncthing, Nextcloud, Drive or anything else. Conflicts are shown, never silently resolved.

  • 15 languages

    English, 中文, हिन्दी, Español, العربية, Français, বাংলা, Português, Русский, Bahasa Indonesia, اردو, Deutsch, 日本語, मराठी, Tiếng Việt.
