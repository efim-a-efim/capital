# Plans

**What it is.** The savings you intend to make: salary at the end of each month, a bonus in December, a tax refund. Plans are not part of your savings and do not change any total. They only extend the projection on the goals.

**What each row shows.** The plan's name, its amount and currency, the date, and *Planned* or *Archived*. The pencil edits, the bin deletes.

**Adding a plan.** Press **+** and enter a name, a currency, an amount and a date. Dates are entered as YYYY-MM-DD or picked from the calendar.

**How plans are applied.** Plans are sorted by date and added up. After each one the app recomputes the allocation with the extra money, applied after every real bucket, to the goals in due-date order. The first date on which a goal's need is covered becomes its closing date, shown on the goal as *Funded in time* or, if it is later than the due date, as *Not funded* with the number of days late.

**Archived plans.** When a plan's date has passed it moves to the **Archived** section, greyed out, and is no longer counted. Either you already moved that money into a bucket and the app sees it there, or the plan did not happen. Edit its date into the future to make it active again, or delete it.

**Currencies.** A plan in another currency is converted with the cached rates. If the rate is missing, the projection is marked incomplete.
