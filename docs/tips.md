# Tip the developer

If you want to tip the developer, use one of the methods below. It won't change anything in the application's functionality, but will help the developer.

{% include tips.html %}

A tip is a transfer from your own wallet to the developer's address. The app and this site take no part in it and cannot see it; nothing is unlocked, recorded or changed. The same addresses are shown in the app under the heart button on the Overview. See [Donations]({{ page.base }}/privacy#donations) in the Privacy Policy.
